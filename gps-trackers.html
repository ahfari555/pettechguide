2. Key Factors to Consider When Buying

Choosing the right device depends heavily on your pet's size and your local cellular coverage:

  • Weight and Size: Ensure the device does not exceed 5% of your pet's total body weight. Smaller cats require lightweight options like specialized cat trackers.
  • Network Coverage: GPS collars rely on cellular networks (LTE-M/5G) to transmit location. Check coverage maps in your area before committing to a yearly plan.
  • Battery Endurance: High-frequency live tracking drains batteries quickly. Look for power-saving Wi-Fi zone features at home.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

Do GPS pet trackers require a monthly subscription?

Yes, because cellular data is used to relay coordinates from your pet's collar to your smartphone in real-time.

Can a GPS tracker replace a microchip?

No. Microchips are permanent passive IDs scanned by vets or shelters, whereas GPS trackers require batteries and cellular connection for live tracking.
