2. How Smart Feeders Help Overweight Pets

Veterinary studies show that over 50% of domestic cats and dogs suffer from weight issues. Automated micro-portions (serving food 4 to 6 times a day instead of two large meals) stabilize blood sugar and prevent binge eating.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

Do smart feeders jam with dry kibble?

Most modern units handle kibble sizes between 2mm to 12mm smoothly. Always check manufacturer size recommendations.
