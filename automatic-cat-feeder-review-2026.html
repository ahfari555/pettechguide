4. Frequently Asked Questions (Expert Answers)

1. Can this automatic feeder be used with wet cat food or canned food?

No, this unit is engineered exclusively for dry kibble. Wet food requires specialized rotating compartment feeders with ice packs to avoid spoiling.

2. What kibble size works best without jamming the dispenser?

Dry kibble sized between 2mm to 12mm in diameter flows smoothly through the dispensing rotor without clogging.

3. What happens if there is a power outage in my home?

The feeder features a dual power backup system. By inserting backup batteries, your cat's scheduled meals will continue dispensing seamlessly even if the wall power fails.

4. Can I record a custom voice message for my cat?
